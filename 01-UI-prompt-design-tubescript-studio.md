### Prompt for YouTube Studio UI Design

This prompt is meticulously crafted for top-tier image generators (e.g., Midjourney v6, DALL-E 3) to create a sophisticated, highly intuitive user interface. It ensures the design is functional, aesthetically beautiful, and tailored specifically for advanced video production.

```markdown
**Master UI/UX Prompt:**

A high-fidelity, comprehensive conceptual screenshot of **TubeScript Studio**, an advanced web applications interface designed strictly for YouTube content creators. The design must be optimized for an ultra-wide desktop display with a clean, professional, sophisticated dark mode aesthetic.

**Visual Style & Palette:**
* **Background:** Dark charcoal grey and deep navy blues.
* **Accents:** Vibrant YouTube-red (primary actions), emerald green (active progress/success), and soft cyan (informational focus).
* **Text:** Clean, modern, highly legible sans-serif typography (e.g., Inter, Roboto) in bright white or soft light grey.

**Application Layout:**

1.  **Header (Top Bar):** 'TubeScript Studio' in bold white text on the upper left. A centered search bar with subtle glow. Circular red profile avatar and quick-action icons (Notifications, New Project) on the upper right.
2.  **Navigation (Left Sidebar):** A sleek vertical sidebar featuring modern icons and text labels: 'Dashboard', 'Projects', 'Analytics', 'Asset Library', and 'Settings'. The active tab ('Projects') is highlighted with a subtle green border and glow.
3.  **Core Workspace (Main Canvas):**

    *   **Progress Stepper (Top Center):** A clean horizontal multi-step progress bar visualizing the production workflow. It consists of connected chevron shapes with subtle gradients:
        *   **Step 1: IDEATION** (Active - highlighted in bright green/white, icon of a lightbulb)
        *   **Step 2: CONTEXT & RESEARCH** (Cyan, icon of a magnifying glass)
        *   **Step 3: STORYBOARD & SCRIPT** (Locked - Grey, icon of a notepad)
        *   **Step 4: REVIEW & LOOP** (Locked - Grey, icon of a circular arrow)

    *   **The 5-Idea Panel (Step 1 Visualization):** The main content area must display the results of your ideation phase. Show five interactive, rounded cards arranged in a grid.
        *   **Card Header:** Each card is clearly numbered (1 to 5) with a distinct 'Angle Type' badge (e.g., *'Myth-Buster', 'Beginner Quick-Start', 'Step-by-Step Tutorial'*).
        *   **Card Content:** Each card must prominently display:
            *   **A "Viral Title Hook"** (e.g., *'The ONE Setting That Kills Your PC Battery'*, *'I Stress-Tested 5 VPNs so You Don’t Have To'*)
            *   **A retention-optimized 3-second hook synopsis.**
            *   **A single "Core Value Proposition" line.**
        *   **Interactive Toggles:** At the bottom of each card, show interactive toggles for 'Format: Shorts 9:16' (Red) or 'Full Video 16:9' and a large green 'SELECT ANGLE' button.

**Interface Polish:**
All elements use clean lines, subtle neon outer glows for active states, rounded corners, and clear separation of data. The design must be high-density yet breathable, emphasizing efficiency and focus for serious video creators.

```
